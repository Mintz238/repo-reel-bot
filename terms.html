Terms of Service — Repo Reel bot

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Repo Reel bot ("the App") is a personal, non-commercial automation tool operated by its owner for their own TikTok account. It creates short videos that introduce trending open-source GitHub repositories and uploads them to the owner's TikTok account as drafts, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

1. Who may use the App

The App is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. By authorizing the App with "Login with TikTok", the account holder confirms they own the TikTok account being connected.

2. What the App does

  • Generates a short video (45–90 seconds) about a public GitHub repository.
  • Uploads the video to the connected TikTok account's inbox as a draft. The account holder reviews, edits and publishes it in the TikTok app.
  • It does not post comments, like, follow, send messages or interact with other users.

3. Content

Videos describe public information from GitHub (repository names, descriptions, star counts and README content) and link to the original repository. The account holder is responsible for reviewing content before publishing and for complying with the TikTok Community Guidelines and TikTok Terms of Service.

4. Access and revocation

The account holder can disconnect the App at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. The App then stops uploading immediately.
